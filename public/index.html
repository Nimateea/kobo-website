Frequently asked questions

What is Kobo?

Kobo is an AI-powered financial companion. It helps you record, understand, plan and track your money in one place, in plain language.

Is Kobo a bank?

No. Kobo is software that helps you understand and manage your money. It is not a bank, lender, investment broker or payment processor.

Does Kobo give financial advice?

Kobo's insights and suggestions are informational, not regulated financial advice. You always stay in control of what you do with them.

How does Kobo use AI?

Kobo looks for patterns, unusual activity and upcoming commitments, then explains why an insight matters and suggests a next step you can accept or ignore.

Is my financial data private?

Privacy is a core design principle. We will explain in plain English what we collect and why, and publish our full Privacy Policy before launch.

Does Kobo work with irregular income?

Yes. Kobo is designed to help you budget around real income, including freelance, creator or other variable earnings.

Who can join, and when?

Access is opening through a waitlist. Join to hear first. We will share launch timing, supported countries and data sources as they are confirmed.
